# OEM imagery — sources and rights (ZT Automotive Collection, internal design exploration)

Collected 23 Nov 2026 from the manufacturers' own public websites and public press newsrooms. No login, no paid download, no access controls bypassed. No stock, fan, social, Wikimedia or dealer-listing sources. None of these images is generated or AI-edited. Large originals were only **downscaled** to 3840 px on the long edge, and the 12Cilindri interior PNG was converted to JPEG. Nothing else was edited or cropped. `preview/` holds 900 px review copies.

## Licensing flags before public use

- **Every image here is copyrighted by its manufacturer (or by the photographer the manufacturer commissioned).** They are for **internal exploration only** (Figma homepage mock-ups for ZT Automotive Collection).
- **No public use without written approval.** That covers a live site, social posts, pitch decks shown outside the team, and print. Approval comes from each OEM or under the ZT dealer/retailer agreement, which usually includes a brand-asset portal and CI rules. Get each marque's approved assets through the dealer channel before launch.
- **Press-newsroom images (Rolls-Royce) are released for editorial/journalistic use**, not for commercial or retail advertising. A dealer homepage is commercial use, so it needs separate approval.
- **Koenigsegg images credit photographer Keno Zache in their file names.** Photographer credit and licensing apply on top of Koenigsegg's own rights.
- **Some cars carry visible number plates**: Spectre (German test plates), Artura green (`Y400 MCL`). Plates usually need retouching or OEM sign-off before public use.
- **Ferrari's resize CDN can upscale to 3840 px, but the true source resolution is about 1920 px**, and the files here are at that native size. Ferrari, McLaren W1 and Artura (except the Spider) are about 1920 px wide. That is fine for mock-ups but soft for a 4K full-bleed hero.
