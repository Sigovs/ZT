Standing rules

- ZT is an automotive group portal. The page needs the group, its people, architecture, service, buying and selling, news and community beside the cars.
- The five marques (McLaren, Rolls-Royce, Koenigsegg, Czinger, Ferrari) stay dominant. The mega menu also carries every secondary brand and dealership, by brand and location, each linking to its own site. Source of truth: https://www.ztautogroup.com/ (content only, never a visual reference).
- Never invent history, services, events, dealerships or figures. Verified content or a visibly marked placeholder.
- Film: muted, looping, inline, with a poster frame and a pause control; only the first visible film preloads; stills on phones and with reduced motion.
- `jarvis/` stays local until Alex approves committing it.
