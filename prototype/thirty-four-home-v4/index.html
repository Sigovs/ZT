The house

A national presence. A personal approach.

ZT Automotive is a family-owned dealership group, founded in 2014 by the Badar Family Office. The ZT Automotive Collection in Charlotte is its luxury and exotic house.

About ZT Automotive

History

  1. 2014 ZT Automotive is founded.
  2. 2015 Fort Walton Beach, Florida: Toyota, Mercedes-Benz, BMW and Mazda.
  3. 2018 Steve Rayman Chevrolet, Georgia.
  4. 2020 Ron Carter in Alvin, Texas: Ford, Chevrolet, CDJR and GMC.
  5. 2024 Tuscumbia and Florence, Alabama: Toyota, Honda, CDJR and Nissan.
  6. 2025 The Tampa area, Florida: Acura and Nissan.
  7. 2026 Charlotte: McLaren and Rolls-Royce Motor Cars join, and the ZT Automotive Collection begins.
