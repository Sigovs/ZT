The house

A national dealership group with the attentiveness of a family-run business.

In ZT Automotive’s own words, from its About page.

ZT Automotive was founded in 2014 and is owned by the Badar Family Office. It began as a single dealership and now runs dealerships in Alabama, Florida, Georgia, North Carolina and Texas.

In 2026 the family office acquired McLaren Charlotte and Rolls-Royce Motor Cars Charlotte and set up the ZT Automotive Collection, its luxury and exotic house. Koenigsegg, Czinger and Midwest Automotive Designs came with it.
